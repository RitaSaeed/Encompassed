Our Programs

Our programs support each stage of early-childhood through structured learning, play, and care. 

Infant Room 1

6 weeks until crawling

Infant Room 1 focuses on early growth through nurturing care and gentle routines. Babies are supported as they begin developing movement, awareness, and interaction with their environment.

Encourages early milestones like eye contact, smiling, and head control
Introduces tummy time using mats, toys, and water play
Engages infants through facial expressions and conversation
Supports sensory exploration and early movement

Infant Room 2

Crawling through walking

We encourage movement, first steps, and early communication through guided play. Children build confidence and social skills as they explore their environment.

 Supports crawling, standing, and first steps
Encourages language development and early communication
Promotes sharing, interaction, and respectful play
Uses walking toys and guided play activities

Tots

13 months to 2.5 years

Our Tots program encourages curiosity, imagination, and hands-on learning through a structured curriculum supported by Brightwheel.

Circle time (date, weather, letters, colors, and shapes)
Creative play, themed activities, and reading
Arts and hands-on learning experiences
Sensory play using sand, play doh, rice, and sensory bins

Early Learners

2.5 years to 3.5 years

Children continue building social, emotional, and academic skills through structured play, creativity, and group learning.

Imaginative and themed activities
Circle time and group interaction
Arts, reading, and exploration
Sensory play and hands-on learning

Preschool / Pre-K

3.5 years to 5 years

This program prepares children for kindergarten through more advanced learning, structured routines, and engaging classroom experiences.

Early literacy and communication skills
Social and emotional development
Classroom readiness and structured learning
Creative activities and sensory exploration
